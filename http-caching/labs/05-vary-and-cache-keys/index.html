Lab 05 — Vary & cache keys

A cache is a map. Vary is the part of the key you forgot to include. Get it wrong in one direction and users see each other's content; get it wrong in the other and your hit rate goes to zero. Both are shipped constantly.

The endpoint here returns a body that depends on the x-lang request header — the classic "content negotiation" setup. Whether it also sends Vary: x-lang is the variable.
