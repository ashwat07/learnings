Lab 04 — Immutable & fingerprinting

How do you cache something for a year and still ship a fix in sixty seconds? You never change a URL's content — you change the URL. This lab simulates deploys against four different naming strategies and counts the bytes each one costs a returning user.

The app being simulated: vendor 300KB, app 80KB, styles 40KB, icons 120KB. Each "deploy" changes only app — the realistic case, and the one where the strategies diverge.
