Lab 04 — Credentials

Cookies cross-origin are governed by two independent systems that people constantly conflate: CORS decides whether your JavaScript may send credentials and read the response; SameSite decides whether the browser attaches the cookie at all. A request can pass one and fail the other, and the error messages look nothing alike.

This lab uses two targets to separate them:
localhost:8081 — cross-origin but same-site (different port only), so SameSite does not block it.
127.0.0.1:8080 — cross-origin and cross-site, so SameSite applies in full.

Set the cookie first, then work down the buttons.
