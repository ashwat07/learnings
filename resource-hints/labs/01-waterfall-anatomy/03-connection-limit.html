03 — connection limit

Twenty images, each with a 400ms server delay, all discoverable immediately. If bandwidth were the constraint they would all finish in ~400ms. They don't.

This server speaks HTTP/1.1, so the browser opens at most six connections per origin. The rest queue — look for the long grey "queue/stall" segments in the waterfall. 20 images ÷ 6 connections × 400ms ≈ 4 rounds ≈ 1.6s.

The second row loads the same images from localhost:8081 — a different origin, therefore a different connection pool, therefore another six in parallel. That is exactly why sites used to shard assets across img1/img2/img3.example.com, and why that practice became an anti-pattern the moment HTTP/2 arrived with one multiplexed connection.
