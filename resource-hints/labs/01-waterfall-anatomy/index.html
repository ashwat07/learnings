Lab 01 — Waterfall anatomy

Four pages, the same total bytes, wildly different load times. Open each one, read its waterfall, and learn to see the two things that actually decide page speed: chain depth and connection contention.

Throttle to Fast 4G in DevTools → Network before you start, and hard-reload each page.

the pages

  • 01-deep-chain.html — five resources, each discovered by the previous one. Same bytes, five sequential round trips.
  • 02-flat.html — the same five resources, all declared in the HTML head. One round trip's worth of latency instead of five.
  • 03-connection-limit.html — 20 images at once over HTTP/1.1. Watch six go at a time.
  • 04-blocking.html — what render-blocking CSS and a parser-blocking script do to discovery of everything after them.

Each page prints its own waterfall and milestones at the bottom. Compare LCP across all four and write the numbers down before reading the README.

what to look for in each waterfall

  • Staircase — each bar starts where the previous one ended. That is a dependency chain, and no amount of bandwidth fixes it.
  • Six at a time — bars starting in groups of six with a long grey queue/stall segment: you are at the HTTP/1.1 per-origin connection limit.
  • Long grey before a bar — queued or stalled, not slow. The network hadn't started yet.
  • A gap with nothing happening — the browser was busy parsing or executing, not waiting on the network. Check the Performance panel, not the Network one.
