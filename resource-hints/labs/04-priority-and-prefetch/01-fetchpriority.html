01 — fetchpriority

The hero below is the LCP element. Eleven thumbnails are declared before it in the HTML, so the preload scanner queues them first and — on a connection-limited origin — they get the sockets. fetchpriority="high" on the hero jumps it to the front.

Load this page, note LCP. Then load ?fix=1, which adds the one attribute, and compare. Throttle to Fast 4G or the difference is noise.
