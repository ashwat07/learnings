03 — prefetch the next page

rel="prefetch" downloads at the lowest priority, into the HTTP cache, for a future navigation. It costs the user bandwidth on a bet that they will click.

  • Prefetch happens at idle priority, so it should not slow this page down. Confirm that in the waterfall rather than assuming it.
  • The prefetched entry lives in the HTTP cache and obeys its caching headers. A response with no-store cannot be prefetched usefully at all — a very common reason prefetch "does nothing".
  • Chrome keeps unused prefetched resources for about five minutes.
  • The grown-up version of this is Speculation Rules (page 04), which can prefetch or fully prerender a whole document, not just subresources.
