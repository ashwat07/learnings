02 — preconnect

Identical page, one extra line in the head. The cross-origin script's row should now show no DNS or connect segment at all — that work already happened, in parallel with the stylesheet.

Caveat to remember: browsers keep an unused preconnected socket open for only about ten seconds. Preconnecting to something you use much later is wasted; preconnecting to six origins "just in case" costs six handshakes and can crowd out the ones that matter.
