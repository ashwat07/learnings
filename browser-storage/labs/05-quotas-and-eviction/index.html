Lab 05 — Quotas & eviction

Everything you store — IndexedDB, Cache Storage, localStorage, OPFS — shares one origin quota. When the disk gets tight the browser evicts whole origins, not individual entries. Your offline app comes back empty, and the user is logged out.

This lab writes real data to your disk. It cleans up after itself, but check Application → Storage when you are done.
