03 — a 10-deep import chain

Ten requests instead of fifty, and it may well be the slowest page in this lab. Each module's dependency cannot even be discovered until the module itself has arrived and been parsed — so you pay one full round trip per level, serially.

Throttle to Fast 3G (≈150ms RTT) and predict the floor before you measure: 10 levels × RTT = the minimum, before a single byte of useful work.

Then try the fix without touching the module structure: add <link rel="modulepreload"> for every step in the chain (commented out below) and measure again. The preload scanner cannot see inside modules — but it can see those link tags.
