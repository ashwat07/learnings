Lab 03 — Long tasks & yielding (INP)

A search box over 40,000 rows, filtered on every keystroke. It works fine. It also has an INP of about a second, and every keystroke is a long task. Your job: keep the behaviour, delete the long tasks, and get INP under 200ms — measured, not guessed.

INP = the worst (roughly p98) interaction latency on the page, measured from the input event to the next paint that reflects it. It has three parts and you have to know which one you're fixing.
