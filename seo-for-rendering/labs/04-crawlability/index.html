Lab 04 — Crawlability

robots.txt, sitemaps, canonicals and pagination: the plumbing that decides which of your URLs get crawled, which get indexed, and which compete with each other. Mostly mechanical — and the failure modes are expensive because they are silent and take weeks to show up.

1. robots.txt tester

Implements the real matching rules: Allow and Disallow are matched by longest path, not by order, with Allow winning ties. Almost everyone assumes first-match-wins.
