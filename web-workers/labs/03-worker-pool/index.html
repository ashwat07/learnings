Lab 03 — Worker pools & cancellation

One worker is a queue. Spawning a worker per job is a fork bomb. A pool of hardwareConcurrency workers is the answer — plus the two things every naive pool lacks: cancellation that actually cancels and backpressure.

hardwareConcurrency here: . Note it is a hint, is often capped for privacy, and says nothing about how many of those cores you are allowed to use.
