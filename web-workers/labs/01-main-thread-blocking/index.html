Lab 01 — Main-thread blocking

Fetch 200,000 rows of JSON, parse them, group and sort them. About 400ms of CPU on a laptop, several seconds on a phone. Do it on the main thread and the page is dead for the duration; do it in a worker and nothing moves except the clock.

Two spinners below. The left one is a CSS animation on the compositor — it keeps going even when the main thread is blocked, which is why it is a terrible loading indicator for this. The right one is driven by requestAnimationFrame, so it freezes exactly when your users' clicks do. Watch the right one.
