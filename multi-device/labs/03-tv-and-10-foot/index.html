Lab 03 — TV & the 10-foot UI

A TV is a 1920px screen viewed from three metres, driven by four arrow keys and an OK button, running on a chipset slower than a 2016 phone. Every one of those facts changes the design.

Click into the panel and use the arrow keys. This is spatial navigation, written by hand — because a TV has no Tab key.
