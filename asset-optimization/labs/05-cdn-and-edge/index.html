Lab 05 — CDN & edge

A CDN is a cache close to the user. That single sentence contains the whole subject: what it caches (the key), how long (the TTL), how you change your mind (the purge), and what it cannot help with (anything uncacheable).

The sandbox has a toy CDN at /api/edge — it caches by pop + path, reports HIT/MISS/EXPIRED with an Age, and can be purged. Enough to reason about the real thing.

Start with MISS then HIT.
