Lab 01 — Images: formats & sizes

Two decisions, in order of impact: how many pixels and which format. Sending a 2000px image to a 400px slot wastes more bytes than any format choice can recover, and it is the more common mistake by a wide margin.

Run node make-images.mjs in asset-optimization/ first — these are real files, encoded on your machine.
