Lab 01 — Service worker lifecycle

Register, update, wait, activate, claim. Every confusing thing about service workers is in these five words, and the confusion is nearly always one of two facts: a new worker waits until every old page is gone, and the page that registered a worker is not controlled by it.

Turn OFF "Update on reload" in Application → Service Workers for this lab, or you will never see the waiting state.
