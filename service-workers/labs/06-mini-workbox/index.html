Lab 06 — Build a mini-Workbox

A routing layer with pluggable strategies — the shape every real service-worker library converges on, because a service worker's job is not "caching", it's a policy per kind of request. The router and two strategies are written; five TODOs are yours.

Open sw.js and read it top to bottom before you start. The interesting design is in the plugin hooks, not the strategies.
