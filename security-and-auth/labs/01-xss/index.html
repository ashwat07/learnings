Lab 01 — XSS & sanitization

Every XSS is the same bug: attacker-controlled data reached a place where the browser expects code. This lab renders the same payload through five sinks and shows which ones execute it — in a sandboxed iframe against a localhost endpoint that exists to be vulnerable.

The payloads are yours to type. Nothing here leaves your machine, and the vulnerable endpoint is bound to localhost.

Pick a payload and render it.
