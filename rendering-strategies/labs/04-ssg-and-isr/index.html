Lab 04 — SSG & ISR

Static generation gives you the fastest possible page and a snapshot that is wrong the moment your data changes. Incremental regeneration keeps the delivery and bounds the wrongness. The only real question is how big your staleness window is and who pays for the refresh.

The sandbox's ISR is literally stale-while-revalidate implemented on the server — same idea as the HTTP header from the caching course, different place.
