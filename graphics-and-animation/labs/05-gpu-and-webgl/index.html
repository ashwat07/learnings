Lab 05 — GPU & WebGL

Canvas 2D draws shapes one at a time on the CPU. WebGL hands the GPU a buffer of vertices and a program, and it draws them all at once. The crossover is somewhere around ten to fifty thousand objects — this lab finds it, with a real shader.
